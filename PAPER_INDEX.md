## Claim Boundary

The series reports frameworks, protocol design, baseline characterization, methods, data architecture, reproducibility materials, and a program-level thesis synthesis.

It does not establish treatment efficacy, clinical response, disease reversal, cure, neurological repair, guaranteed recovery, controlled-substance how-to, or software validation.
