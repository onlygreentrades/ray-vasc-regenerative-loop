# External Reviewer Policy

**Version:** 1.1  
**Date:** 2026-06-06  
**Author:** Logan Henderson

## Purpose

External review is invited to improve scientific rigor, claim discipline, safety boundaries, reproducibility, and implementation clarity.

## Reviewer Scope

Reviewers may comment on:

- Manuscript logic and target-journal fit.
- Evidence grading.
- Protocol feasibility.
- Measurement architecture.
- Data dictionary completeness.
- Ethics and privacy boundaries.
- Controlled-substance governance language.

Reviewers should not request or receive raw private clinical records through this public repository.

## Contributor Standard

Contributors to clinical, protocol, biomarker, neurophysiology, ethics, or controlled-substance sections should identify relevant expertise or role. General formatting and copy-editing contributions may be accepted when they do not alter scientific, clinical, legal, or safety meaning.
