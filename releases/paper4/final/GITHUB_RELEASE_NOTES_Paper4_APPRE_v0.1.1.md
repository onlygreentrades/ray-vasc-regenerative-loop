## RAY-VASC v2.3 — Paper 4 APPRE Schema-and-Rules Methods Architecture

This release publishes Paper 4 of the RAY-VASC Regenerative Loop research sequence:

**APPRE: A Schema-and-Rules Framework for Adaptive Measurement, Confounder Annotation, and Response-Policy Documentation in Complex Neurovascular Disease**

APPRE v0.1.1 is released as a public methods/data-architecture artifact for repeated measures, confounder annotation, adverse-event capture, missingness handling, provenance, synthetic-data testing, and versioned response-policy documentation.

APPRE is not validated artificial intelligence, not clinical decision support, not Software as a Medical Device, not a treatment optimizer, and not a longevity product. It is a transparent schema-and-rules framework for organizing research documentation in RAY-VASC-style N-of-1 and small-series complex neurovascular protocols.

Included in this release:

- Paper 4 manuscript in DOCX, PDF, and Markdown
- APPRE schema CSV and JSON files
- Synthetic APPRE datasets
- Validation rules
- Privacy/release checklist
- Reproducibility checklist
- FHIR conceptual crosswalk
- Missingness, confounder, and adverse-event vocabularies
- Response-policy rules
- Analysis notebook templates
- OSF/GitHub release notes
- Governance and future software/AI boundary documents

Controlled-substance exposure, if present in future lawful research implementations, is documented only as as-administered observational data under separate lawful, clinician-governed, ethics-approved oversight. APPRE does not recommend, adjust, tune, or optimize controlled-substance exposure.

Not medical advice. Not legal advice.
