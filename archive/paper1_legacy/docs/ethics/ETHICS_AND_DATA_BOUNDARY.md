# Ethics and Data Boundary

**Version:** 1.1  
**Date:** 2026-06-06  
**Author:** Logan Henderson

## Status

This repository is a publication-only open-source research framework. It is not medical advice, legal advice, a clinical protocol for self-use, a supplement brand, a retreat, a clinic, or a commercial treatment service.

## Controlled-Substance Boundary

Controlled-substance content is restricted to high-level research design, safety monitoring, and as-administered exposure documentation under lawful clinician-governed and ethics-approved settings.

This repository must not contain:

- Sourcing instructions.
- Preparation instructions.
- Administration instructions.
- Self-use instructions.
- Unsupervised dosing guidance.
- Instructions that enable illegal acquisition or use.

APPRE is explicitly prohibited from tuning controlled-substance dosing outside formal clinical or research governance. Controlled-substance exposure fields, if present in future approved studies, are recorded only as clinician- or program-administered, as-administered data.

## Research Gate

OSF registration should precede external prospective data collection. IRB or ethics review is required when identifiable participants, prospective human-subject data, clinical interventions, or controlled-substance exposure are involved.
