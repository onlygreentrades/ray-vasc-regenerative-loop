## Manuscript Role

Paper 2 is the protocol and methods manuscript for the RAY-VASC Regenerative Loop research program.

It translates the Paper 1 framework into a prospective observational N-of-1 and small-series protocol architecture.

It defines:

- Eligibility and site capability.
- Study design and reporting standards.
- Two-cycle Compounding Plasticity Architecture.
- Phase 1 Baseline/Pre-Load.
- Optional Phase 2 Governed Plasticity Window.
- Phase 3 Sustain/Integration.
- Tiered outcomes and measurement architecture.
- Serial EEG and biomarker synchronization.
- APPRE schema and rules.
- Safety monitoring, adverse-event capture, and stopping rules.
- Open science, reproducibility, and repository governance.

## Relationship to Paper 1

Paper 1 defines the parent framework.

Paper 2 operationalizes that framework into a protocol structure for feasibility, safety, longitudinal measurement, confounder annotation, signal detection, and future study scaling.

Paper 2 should be read with Paper 1 when evaluating the full RAY-VASC Regenerative Loop architecture.

## Public Boundary

This protocol manuscript reports no human-subject outcomes.

It is not an efficacy trial.

It does not establish causality, treatment benefit, disease modification, tissue regeneration, brain repair, vascular lesion reversal, PSP reversal, dementia reversal, or recovery guarantees.

It does not provide medical advice or legal advice.

It does not provide controlled-substance sourcing, preparation, dosing, administration, conversion, or self-use instructions.

Phase 2, if activated in any future implementation, may occur only under separate lawful, clinician-governed, ethics-approved oversight and is documented only as as-administered observational exposure.

APPRE does not recommend, adjust, optimize, or tune controlled-substance exposure.

## Implementation Boundary

External prospective implementation requires applicable consent, privacy review, clinician oversight, and IRB review, ethics review, or formal determination before enrollment.

OSF registration is required before external prospective participant data collection.

Public sharing is limited to de-identified protocols, forms, schemas, synthetic datasets, analysis notebooks, data dictionaries, and evidence maps unless separate consent, privacy review, de-identification, and ethics governance authorize additional sharing.
