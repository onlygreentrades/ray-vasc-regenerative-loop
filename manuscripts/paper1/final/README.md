## Manuscript Role

Paper 1 is the conceptual and framework manuscript for the RAY-VASC Regenerative Loop research program.

It defines:

- The residual adaptive capacity hypothesis.
- The Regenerative Loop.
- APPRE, the Algorithmic Personalization Predictive Response Engine.
- RAY-VASC as the originating measurement spine.
- The Three-Phase Plasticity Architecture:
  - Phase 1: Pre-Load.
  - Phase 2: Governed Plasticity Window.
  - Phase 3: Sustain/Integration.
- The claim boundary for open-source neuroplasticity protocol design.

## Relationship to Paper 2

Paper 2 operationalizes this framework into a prospective observational N-of-1 and small-series protocol.

Paper 1 should be cited as the parent framework manuscript when using or adapting the Paper 2 protocol.

## Public Boundary

This manuscript reports no new clinical outcomes.

It does not claim treatment efficacy.

It does not claim disease reversal, brain repair, tissue regeneration, vascular lesion reversal, PSP reversal, dementia reversal, or recovery guarantees.

It does not provide medical advice or legal advice.

It does not provide controlled-substance sourcing, preparation, dosing, administration, conversion, or self-use instructions.

Any controlled-substance research layer discussed in this manuscript is restricted to lawful, clinician-governed, ethics-reviewed, as-administered observational contexts.
