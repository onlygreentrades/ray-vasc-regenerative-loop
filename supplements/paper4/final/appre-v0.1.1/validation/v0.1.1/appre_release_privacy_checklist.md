# APPRE Public Release Privacy Checklist v0.1.1

- [ ] No direct identifiers.
- [ ] No medical record numbers.
- [ ] No full dates of birth.
- [ ] No addresses, phone numbers, or emails.
- [ ] No raw clinical notes or screenshots.
- [ ] No private imaging/lab reports.
- [ ] No unapproved individual-level clinical outcome data.
- [ ] No controlled-substance how-to content.
- [ ] Synthetic data are clearly synthetic and do not mimic a real person.
- [ ] Release manifest and checksums generated.
