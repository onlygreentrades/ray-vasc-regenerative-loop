# Regulatory Boundary

This repository is for research planning, documentation, protocol architecture, and open-science reproducibility only.

It is not clinical care, medical advice, legal advice, a treatment recommendation, a supplement protocol, a psychedelic protocol, or a commercial product repository.

Paper 3 is a baseline characterization and measurement-feasibility case report. It does not report intervention outcomes, treatment response, clinical efficacy, recovery, reversal, repair, cure, or disease modification.

Controlled-substance content, where referenced, is limited to high-level research-governance architecture and as-administered exposure documentation. No sourcing, preparation, dosing, administration, conversion, or self-use instructions are provided.

External implementation requires local ethics review, IRB review, or formal determination as applicable, plus informed consent or legally authorized representative consent, privacy review, adverse-event procedures, and appropriate clinical oversight.
