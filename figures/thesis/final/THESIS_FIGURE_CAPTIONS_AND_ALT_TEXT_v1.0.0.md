# RAY-VASC Thesis Figure Sources v1.0.0

These three files are the full-resolution standalone source figures corresponding to the three figures embedded in `RAY-VASC_Thesis_v1.0.0.docx`.

## Figure 1 — Neuropathic Repair-Remap Hypothesis

**File:** `THESIS_F01_Neuropathic_Repair-Remap_Hypothesis_Four-Operation_Model.png`

**Caption:** The Neuropathic Repair-Remap Hypothesis formal model. Reinforce, remap, prevent, and re-link converge on the study of residual adaptive capacity in surviving neural systems.

**Alt text:** Four colored operations surround a central residual-adaptive-capacity block: reinforce substrate priming and terrain stabilization; remap network-state modulation; prevent continued central and safety-gating burden; and re-link functional reconnection and network access.

## Figure 2 — Three-Phase Plasticity Architecture

**File:** `THESIS_F02_Three-Phase_Plasticity_Architecture.png`

**Caption:** Three-Phase Plasticity Architecture: Pre-Load, Governed Plasticity Window, and Sustain and Integration.

**Alt text:** A left-to-right three-stage research architecture. Phase 1 stabilizes terrain and establishes interpretable baseline data. Phase 2 represents an optional governed plasticity window under separate lawful and clinical oversight. Phase 3 sustains measurement, response-policy documentation, and integration.

## Figure 3 — APPRE Inputs, Filters, Responseome, and Policy Decision

**File:** `THESIS_F03_APPRE_Inputs_Filters_Responseome_Policy_Decision.png`

**Caption:** APPRE translates subject inputs through safety and eligibility filters into a longitudinal responseome and versioned response-policy decision.

**Alt text:** Four connected columns show inputs, participant-specific filters, the responseome, and a policy decision. The diagram emphasizes transparent rules, confounder capture, adverse-event documentation, missingness, and versioned policy updates.

## Boundary

These figures describe research architecture and hypotheses. They do not establish treatment efficacy, disease modification, neurological repair, reversal, recovery, cure, or guaranteed benefit.
