# Privacy and Public Data Boundary

This repository is a publication-only research documentation repository.

Do not upload raw clinical records, medical record numbers, full dates of birth, clinical screenshots, lab screenshots, imaging reports, addresses, private communications, source-lock ledgers, consent documents, facility identifiers, or identifiable source documents.

Public materials may include manuscripts, protocol templates, forms, schemas, synthetic examples, analysis templates, release notes, and governance documentation.

Paper 3 is a public baseline characterization and measurement-feasibility manuscript. It does not include raw RAY-001 clinical records, direct identifiers, portal screenshots, full dates of birth, medical record numbers, addresses, facility identifiers, private source-lock files, or consent/governance files.

Any future sharing of individual-level clinical data requires separate consent, privacy review, de-identification, and ethics governance.
